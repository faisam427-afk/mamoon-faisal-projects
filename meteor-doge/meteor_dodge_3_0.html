Meteor Dodge 3.0 - Game Design Document

Level 3 Boss Combat & Ammo System Specifications

1. Executive Summary

Meteor Dodge 3.0 converts the original evasion loop into a dynamic retro space shooter with Level 3 Boss Battles and Laser Ordnance powered by golden energy orbs.

2. Weapons & Ammo Logistics

  • Laser Blaster (`F` Key): Fires high-speed cyan plasma bolts that disintegrate meteors and damage the Level 3 Dreadnought Boss.
  • Orbs as Ammo: Floating golden orbs grant +5 Laser Ammo when harvested.
  • Slower Asteroids: Asteroid velocity across all levels has been tuned to moderate speeds (1.0 – 3.2 px/frame) allowing tactical aiming.
  • Bottom-Left Health Bar: Monitors player ship hull shield integrity (3 Hits with invincibility frames).
  • Bottom-Right Ammo Gauge: Visual breakdown of available laser shots.
